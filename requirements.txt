Requirements
* Smaller 2 pin buttons
* S3-Wroom-1
* Mosfets and terminals for e-matches
   * 3 matches (3 positive, 1 ground terminal)
   * Continuity test per ematch with high ohm external pull ups for voltage divider.
* Terminal for uart for GCS
* Sensors
   * Use SPI unless they don’t support it
   * BMP 390 - barometer
   * LSM6DSOX + LIS3MDL - High Precision 9 DOF IMU
   * PA1616D - GPS
   * ADXL375 - High G 6 DOF IMU
* 1 gbit flash chip / High capacity SD card
